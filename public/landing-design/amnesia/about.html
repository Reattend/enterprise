§ About

Why a memory company writes about forgetting.

These essays are published by Reattend, which makes organizational memory software. We write them because the problem is older and larger than any product, and most of what gets said about it online is either vendor copy or one recycled statistic.

§ Charter

What this is, and what it is not.

What this is

  • A plain-language guide to the published research on organizational forgetting, knowledge loss and corporate amnesia.
  • Figures with their sources attached. Every number names the paper, book or report it comes from.
  • Two free tools, a self-assessment and a cost estimator. Both run in your browser and store nothing.
  • A reading list of the books and papers worth your time.

What this is not

  • Original field research. We have not run our own studies. Where an essay makes an argument rather than reports a finding, it says so.
  • A validated test. The diagnostic is a structured way to think about exposure, not a psychometric instrument.
  • Neutral about software. We build a product in this space, and the essays say so where it is relevant.
  • Gated. Nothing here asks for your email address.
