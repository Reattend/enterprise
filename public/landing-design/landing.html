A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Still curious? Let’s talk ↗

Is Reattend for me or for my team?＋

Both. Use a personal workspace to connect your own notes, ideas, and memories. Teams can use shared organizational memory for decisions, handoffs, and project context, with access controls for different people and roles.

How is this different from another notes app?＋

Notes are the starting point. Reattend is built around connecting captured knowledge, recalling it in conversation, preserving the context behind decisions, and helping that knowledge stay useful as people and projects change.

What can I put in my second brain?＋

Start with typed notes, pasted text, links, voice capture, or uploaded documents and images. Connected tools can bring in additional context. Visit the capture overview and integration directory for supported workflows.

Can I see where an answer came from?＋

Conversational recall connects answers to source memories so you can inspect the original context. The animated examples on this page use sample content to show how that experience works.

What happens when someone leaves the team?＋

Knowledge transfer and exit-interview workflows help capture relevant experience before a transition. Decisions and project context remain part of the organization’s memory, governed by its permissions.

How do I get started?＋

Create a workspace and capture your first thought, or explore plans and pricing. For organization-specific deployment and access requirements, book a demo.
